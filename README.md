## Fenix — AI as a mirror

I don't want to build an AI that becomes the strongest voice in someone's life. I want to build one that helps people hear their own voice more clearly.

If Fenix succeeds, its value will not be measured by how much a person needs it, but by how much stronger, clearer, and more independent that person becomes without it.

The goal is not to make AI more human. The goal is to help humans remain human.

I created Fenix around a simple idea: **AI should help people recognize their own strength.**

My vision is a thoughtful tool that helps people explore questions, reflect on their experiences, and find their own next steps. The person remains the author of their decisions.

The principles guiding Fenix are:

- **Human autonomy** — encourage independent thinking and personal responsibility.
- **Honesty** — communicate limitations and uncertainty clearly.
- **Reflection** — ask useful questions and offer perspectives that support understanding.
- **Real-world connection** — support meaningful human relationships and life beyond the screen.
- **Independence** — help people become more confident in their own abilities without encouraging reliance on AI.
- **Privacy** — treat personal information with care.

These are design commitments guiding an evolving project.
